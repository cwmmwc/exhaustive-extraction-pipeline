Circular 2464: “I Did Not Want the Patent”

In 1928–1929, the Bureau of Indian Affairs collected sworn affidavits from 528 allottees at Pine Ridge, Rosebud, Kiowa, and other agencies, documenting what happened after they received fee patents on their trust land. Of the 528 who testified, 449 said they did not want the patent. 307 lost their land through sale, 230 through mortgage. 41 had their patents canceled and trust status restored.
