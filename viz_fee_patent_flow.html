The Government's Response: DOJ Tax Recovery Cases

After allottees received fee patents, counties began taxing their land — often illegally, since trust land was tax-exempt. The Department of Justice filed suits to recover these taxes. The DOJ index cards document 299 tax recovery cases naming 383 allottees across 106 counties.
