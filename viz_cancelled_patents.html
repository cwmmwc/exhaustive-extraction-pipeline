Cancelled Fee Patents: The Government’s Reversal

Between 1916 and 1931, the federal government cancelled 654 fee patents across 66 tribes and 14 states. Of these, 595 were substantive cancellations (under the 1927 or 1931 Acts, acceptance refused, or erroneously issued) and 59 were administrative corrections (name changes, duplicate patents, description corrections). The substantive cancellations are evidence that the government itself acknowledged the patents should not have been issued.

Cross-referencing against the 528 Circular 2464 affidavits identifies 95 allottees who both testified about forced patenting and had their patents cancelled — the complete arc from dispossession to official acknowledgment.
